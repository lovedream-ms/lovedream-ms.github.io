不等 ​ASSERT_NE(val1, val2)​ ​EXPECT_NE(val1, val2)​ ​val1 != val2​
小于 ​ASSERT_LT(val1, val2)​ ​EXPECT_LT(val1, val2)​ ​val1 < val2​
